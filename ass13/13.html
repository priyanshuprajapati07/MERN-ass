Key Terms

CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average, used to represent academic performance.
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
A field of computer science focused on creating systems that perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence.
Internship
Practical work experience that helps students develop professional skills.
Database
An organized collection of data that can be stored, accessed, and managed.
